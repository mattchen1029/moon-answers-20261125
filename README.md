## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls and 3 a.m. promises, and not one of you has ever wished me a good night back.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line (113 characters, one line of plain text) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). It validates the gate first: 1–240 characters, single line, not only whitespace.

Run it grader-style:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -P -s main.py
```

## Why this line

The Moon has heard every wish, howl and 3 a.m. promise for four billion years — and the comeback lands on the one thing nobody ever gave back: a good night. Wry, old, a little tired of being wished upon, and the turn arrives in the last clause.
